### Colorful InFit edit prompt

Edit the supplied InFit concept illustration into a clearer, colorful UX explanation for a portfolio. Keep the same two-screen purpose: Today meal overview and Review meal with editable portions. This remains a design concept, not an actual screenshot. Redesign the visual with upright, nearly front-facing panels, minimal perspective and large clean sans-serif type readable at small size. Use a pale warm cream background, white panels, soft fresh green for the calorie ring and Save meal button, muted orange and coral for small macro indicators. The colors should be tasteful, professional and friendly, not neon. Keep generous spacing and gentle shadows. Include only short clear headings 'Today' and 'Review meal', small Protein / Carbs / Fat labels, breakfast and lunch entries 'Idli', 'Sambar', 'Masala dosa', plus simple portion minus/plus controls and 'Save meal'. Remove date labels, extra menus, tiny descriptive text, bottom navigation clutter and dark black button. Show no AI camera scanner, voice controls, successful recognition or claimed nutrition measurements. No logos, people, device hardware or watermark. One wide 16:9 image. The point is to help a visitor understand meal overview then portion review.

Created 12 October 2026. Built-in ChatGPT image generation was used for the two concept images. No paid API or video generation was used.
